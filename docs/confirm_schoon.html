Goal

The goal was to submit and secure a STCB grant application for feasibility testing of PHA materials in shoe components.

What happened

A STCB grant application was prepared and submitted via Innovein, covering the tested components (toe-bump, heel-counter, outsole), materials (PHA thermoplastic sheets and PHA–natural rubber composites) and validation tests. It included input from Helian Polymers and Paques Biomaterials on material feasibility. RVO granted the €25,000 subsidy, with the advance paid within two weeks. Project period: 1 August 2026 to 31 July 2027.

Not investigated: Paques Biomaterials participation, detailed cost breakdown.

Result

Achieved. The STCB grant was successfully submitted, approved, and funded.

Learnings

Start the application process early — timing was critical to secure the budget before closure.
